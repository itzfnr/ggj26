# Maskgician

# About
Maskigican is a match-3 RPG where magic wears many faces.

Long ago, the land of Vaeloria was shattered by a cabal of evil magicians. Their masks twisted magic itself, plunging the world into chaos. Now, a century later, you rise as Vane, a lone Maskigican - one who channels ancient power through enchanted masks.

Travel across a hand-drawn world map, confront powerful enemies, and engage in strategic match-3 combat where every move matters. Match elemental masks to unleash devastating attacks, conjure shields, heal wounds, and turn the tide of battle -but beware: your enemies strike back.

Each mask tells a story. Each battle is a masquerade of deception, strategy, and power. Enemies can attack too - and they will exploit your mistakes. Mastering how and when to use each mask is the key to survival.

Will you master the masks… or be consumed by them?
Choose your matches carefully.
Your power lies in the mask you wear.

# Tech Notes
This game was built using the Universal Windows Platform (UWP), making it compatible with Windows 10/11 and UWP-enabled devices—including Xbox consoles.

While not an official Microsoft Store release, the experience is optimized for a console environment. If you have an Xbox in Developer Mode, you can sideload the game to experience it as intended. Please use the files in the 'UWP Build' folder for console deployment, or the 'Windows Build' folder for standard PC play.
